MeterX Privacy Policy

Effective date: 17 October 2026

MeterX, provided by MosquitoDevs, helps you track utility meters, readings, payments, vehicles, and renewal records. This policy explains how MeterX handles your information.

Information we collect

When you create an account, we collect your username and a securely hashed version of your password. When you use cloud sync, we process the data you enter, including meter identifiers and readings, payment amounts and method names, vehicle registration details, service or renewal records, notes, and app-generated record identifiers and timestamps.

The app also stores an authentication token, reminder preferences, and a local copy of your records on your device. MeterX does not include advertising SDKs and does not collect precise location, contacts, photos, or financial account credentials.

How we use and share information

We use this information only to provide account access, cloud backup and synchronization, reminders, support, security, and service maintenance. We do not sell your personal data. Data is processed by infrastructure providers used to operate MeterX, including Render for the application service and MongoDB Atlas for database hosting, only as needed to provide the service.

Security and retention

MeterX uses HTTPS for data in transit, encrypted Android storage for the saved session, and password hashing on the server. No system is completely secure. We retain account and cloud data while your account remains active. When you delete your account, the active account and associated cloud records are removed; limited backup copies may remain temporarily until service-provider backup cycles expire.

Your choices

You can export your records from the app. You can permanently delete your account and associated cloud data in Settings → Account → Delete account and data, or use the web account-deletion page. Uninstalling the app removes its local data from that device, subject to Android backup settings.

Children

MeterX is a general utility-record app and is not directed to children under 13.
