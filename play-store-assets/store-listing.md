## Full description

MeterX keeps everyday utility and vehicle records organized in one private, easy-to-use app.

Track electricity, water, and gas meters with dated readings, usage history, billing status, payment records, and custom fields. Electricity meters can include a free-unit allowance, with clear progress and warning states as usage approaches the limit.

Keep vehicle information alongside pollution certificates, insurance, and service history. Record renewal dates, amounts, odometer readings, and notes, then review upcoming and overdue items from the vehicle dashboard.

Key features:
• Track electricity, water, and gas meters
• Record and edit dated readings
• Monitor usage between readings
• Track bills, payments, and payment methods
• Add custom fields for individual meters
• Manage vehicles and odometer readings
• Track pollution, insurance, and service renewals
• Receive optional daily reading reminders
• Export and import a portable JSON backup
• Sync your records securely across devices
• Continue viewing local data when offline

MeterX does not contain ads. Your records remain under your control and can be exported or permanently deleted from Settings.

## Data-safety inventory

Data collected for app functionality and cloud sync:

- Account management: username and app-generated user ID
- User content: meter numbers, consumer numbers, readings, custom fields, notes
- Financial info: user-entered bill/payment amounts and payment-method labels (not bank or card credentials)
- App activity/records: vehicle registration details, odometer readings, and maintenance/renewal records
- Authentication: password is transmitted securely and stored only as a one-way hash; session token is encrypted on the device

Data is encrypted in transit, is not sold, is not used for advertising, and can be deleted in the app or at the public account-deletion URL. Render and MongoDB Atlas act as service providers to operate the backend and database.
